Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

CozyTV+ Android purchases are processed only through Google Play. No external payment is accepted.

1. Free trial

No payment is taken for the 15-day trial. If you do not purchase Premium when it ends, you are not charged and app access is suspended.

2. Purchase types

1-Year Premium Access and Lifetime Premium Access are one-time in-app purchases. There is no subscription or automatic renewal.

3. Google Play refunds

Refund requests are governed by current Google Play rules. Where eligible, you may request a refund through your Google Play order history within 48 hours of purchase. Timing and eligibility may vary by country, product and Google Play’s decision.

4. After 48 hours and technical issues

If Google Play does not offer a refund option or your purchase entitlement did not activate, contact htok2005@gmail.com with your order number. Requests are reviewed under Google Play rules, transaction status, technical records and applicable consumer law.

5. Restore purchases

If you reinstalled the app or changed devices, first try restoring the purchase using the same Google Account. If you reached the three-device limit, remove an old device before adding a new one.
