This policy applies to paid CozyTV+ Premium software access. CozyTV+ sells software features only and does not provide, sell, host, or distribute television channels, playlists, movies, or other media content.

Free trial

Supported devices may receive a 15-day free trial. No purchase is required for the trial. If a paid plan is started through a checkout that includes a trial, the checkout terms shown before purchase govern when billing begins.

Yearly subscription

The Yearly plan ($8.99/year) is a recurring subscription. You may cancel a recurring subscription at any time through the customer portal or cancellation method provided by Lemon Squeezy, our authorized merchant of record identified on your receipt. Cancellation stops future renewals. Premium access normally remains available until the end of the current paid billing period.

Lifetime Premium

Lifetime Premium is a one-time purchase ($12.99) that grants a non-expiring software entitlement for the designated device. It does not automatically renew. Approved refunds or payment reversals revoke the associated Lifetime entitlement.

To request a refund, contact the merchant of record using the support or transaction link included in your receipt. You may also contact CozyTV+ support at htok2005@gmail.com with your purchase reference so we can help route your request.

Technical issues

If CozyTV+ does not work as described on a supported device, contact support with your device model, platform version, CozyTV+ version, and a description of the issue. We will make reasonable efforts to diagnose the problem before or alongside any refund request.
