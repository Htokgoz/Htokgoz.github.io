CozyTV+ Data Deletion Request

Applies to: CozyTV+ (Android, LG webOS, iOS)

Developer: Hakan Tokgöz (Cosmo Online)

Fast Summary: CozyTV+ does not require or collect user accounts (such as username, password, or email registration). However, if you wish to delete all pseudonymous backend data and verification records associated with your installation, you may submit a request using the instructions below.

1. Overview and Scope

This Data Deletion policy explains how users of CozyTV+, developed by Hakan Tokgöz, can request the deletion of data associated with their application usage and backend services in accordance with Google Play's user data policies.

For complete details on what information CozyTV+ processes, please review our full Privacy Policy.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your backend data, please submit an email request following these steps:

  1. Send an email to: htok2005@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: CozyTV+ Data Deletion Request
  3. Include your CozyTV+ Device Code in the body of the message (e.g., CZTV-XXXX-XXXX).

    Note: You can locate your Device Code inside the CozyTV+ application by navigating to Settings > Device / Account Info. Because CozyTV+ does not store personal email or profile accounts, your Device Code is the required identifier to locate your backend records.

3. Types of Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon receiving and verifying your request, we will delete the following records from our backend databases (Cloudflare D1):

  • Device Code: The pseudonymous identifier assigned to your installation.
  • Device Identifier Hash: Stable device identifier references or anonymized hardware hashes linked to your device.
  • Trial Status: Records tracking trial activation, duration, and status.
  • Premium Entitlement Status: Backend entitlement records granting active access to premium features.
  • Google Play Purchase Verification Records: Purchase tokens and transaction verification records linked to the device.

4. Types of Data That May Be Retained

In certain circumstances, minimal records may be retained or cannot be immediately purged:

  • Fraud Prevention & Abuse Mitigation: Cryptographically hashed references necessary to prevent abuse of free trials or fraudulent purchase chargebacks.
  • Legal, Accounting & Financial Compliance: Records required to comply with statutory legal obligations, tax filings, or accounting standards where mandatory under applicable law.
  • Minimal Audit Records: Anonymized timestamps indicating that a deletion request was received and fulfilled.

5. Processing Timeline

Data deletion requests are typically processed within 30 calendar days of receiving your email with a valid Device Code. Once deletion is completed, a confirmation email will be sent back to you.

6. Important Considerations Before Requesting Deletion

  • Entitlement Reset: Deleting your backend records removes your entitlement status from CozyTV+ servers. If you have an active lifetime or active subscription entitlement, deleting your backend record will unlink it from your device.
  • Google Play Subscriptions: Deleting backend application data does not automatically cancel recurring subscriptions billed through Google Play. To cancel an active Google Play subscription, please visit the Google Play Store app > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions on your Android device.
  • Local Device Data: Playlists, credentials, and playback histories stored locally on your device can be removed directly by selecting Reset App in CozyTV+ settings or clearing the app storage in your operating system's settings.

7. Privacy Policy and Inquiries

For more information on our data handling practices, visit our Privacy Policy.

If you have any questions regarding this policy or data privacy, please contact the developer at htok2005@gmail.com.
