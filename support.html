Common topics

Restore a purchase

Make sure the Google Account used for purchase is active on the device, then use the restore-purchase option in the app.

Three-device limit

If you reached the limit, remove a device you no longer use from device management and add the new device.

A playlist or stream does not open

Check the source address, internet connection and your provider’s simultaneous-connection limit. CozyTV+ does not provide content or an IPTV service.

Movie, series, audio or subtitles are missing

These features depend on the data and stream format supplied by your source and on device support.
