Before contacting support

  • Confirm that your device has an active internet connection.
  • Confirm that your playlist, Xtream-compatible account, EPG source, or direct stream works with your provider.
  • Restart CozyTV+ and your device.
  • If the problem concerns activation, include your CozyTV+ Device ID if shown in the App.
  • Include the device platform (LG webOS, Android, or iOS), App version, and a short description of the issue.

Important

CozyTV+ does not provide IPTV subscriptions, playlists, channels, movies, or series. Questions about your IPTV subscription, channel availability, account credentials, or provider-side outages should be directed to your IPTV provider.
