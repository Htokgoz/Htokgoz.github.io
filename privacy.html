1.1 About CozyTV+ and Content Responsibility

CozyTV+ is a smart media player designed to play and organize audiovisual media from sources provided directly by the user. CozyTV+ does not provide, host, sell, broadcast, or distribute television channels, streams, playlists, or any media content. Users are solely responsible for ensuring they have legitimate rights and licenses to access any content or streams they configure.

1.2 Data Stored Exclusively on Your Device (Not Collected)

To provide playback functionality, CozyTV+ stores the following configuration data locally on your device (using local device storage and IndexedDB). This data is never transmitted to CozyTV+ servers:

  • Playlists and Account Credentials: M3U/M3U8 URLs, Xtream server addresses, usernames, passwords, and XMLTV/EPG URLs.
  • Preferences & Playback State: Favorite channels, custom channel ordering, audio/subtitle selections, playback progress, reminders, and watchlists.
  • Parental Controls: Salted PIN hashes and channel restriction flags.

Temporary cached media and guide data may be removed using Clear Cache. User-added accounts, playlists, credentials, preferences, favorites, and viewing history remain on the device until you use Reset App in settings, clear the application's storage through the operating system, or uninstall CozyTV+.

1.3 Data Processed on Backend Servers (Collected Data)

CozyTV+ operates without personal user account registration. To manage trial periods, provide technical support, deliver the appropriate interface layout, and manage Premium feature entitlements without requiring personal accounts, our backend processes minimal pseudonymous device information and technical specifications:

A. Device Identifiers and Technical Specifications

  • Android Pseudonymized Identifier: On Android, the application uses a one-way, pseudonymized device identifier derived from the stable device identifier provided by Android. The stable identifier provided by Android is transmitted securely over HTTPS and processed ephemerally only to derive a one-way pseudonymized device identifier. The raw Android identifier is never persisted in the backend database and is never logged. CozyTV+ never collects personal device numbers or hardware profile hashes.
  • LG webOS Identifier: On LG webOS, LGUDID is accessed only after your explicit in-app consent solely to enforce single-use trial rules and restore paid entitlements.
  • CozyTV+ Device Code: A pseudonymous identifier (e.g. CZTV-XXXX-XXXX) generated by our backend and associated with your device entitlement.
  • Technical Device Specifications: During device registration and periodic metadata synchronization, the following technical device specifications may be transmitted to our backend:
    • Device Category: Form factor classification (phone, tablet, Android TV, Google TV, or LG webOS TV).
    • Operating System Version: Operating system release version (e.g. Android release version, webOS version).
    • Android SDK/API Level: The numerical Android SDK/API level (e.g. API 31, 33, 34).
    • Device Manufacturer: Hardware manufacturer name (e.g. Sony, Google, Samsung, LG).
    • Device Model: Hardware model name or designation (e.g. BRAVIA 4K, Pixel 8, Galaxy Tab S8, OLED55C1).
    • Platform: Underlying platform identifier (android, webos).
    • Application Version: CozyTV+ installed application version string (e.g. 2.0.8).

B. Purposes of Technical Data Processing

These technical device specifications and identifiers are processed strictly and exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Ensuring technical compatibility with your specific device and operating system version.
  • Presenting the appropriate user interface layout tailored specifically for phone, tablet, or TV form factors.
  • Providing technical customer support, diagnosing errors, and troubleshooting playback or integration issues.
  • Managing single-use free trial periods and verifying paid Premium feature entitlements.
  • Accurately identifying and verifying the specific device associated with user-provided device codes in administrative management and customer support.

C. Explicit Limitations — What We Do NOT Collect

  • No Telephony or Network Identifiers: We do NOT collect IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, IMSI, or SIM serial numbers.
  • No Hardware Serial Numbers: We do NOT collect device serial numbers or CPU/hardware profiling hashes.
  • No Advertising Identifiers: We do NOT collect Google Advertising ID (GAID/AAID) or other advertising tracking identifiers.
  • No Raw Android Identifier Persisted: The raw Android identifier is transmitted securely over HTTPS and processed ephemerally to derive a pseudonymized identifier; it is never persisted in our backend database and is never logged. Only the resulting one-way, pseudonymized identifier is stored.

D. Access Products and Entitlements (Not Subscriptions)

CozyTV+ products are not subscriptions and do not renew automatically. In the Android edition, the application offers strictly one-time purchases:

  • 1-Year Premium Access: A one-time purchase granting 365 days of Premium access from the purchase date. Access concludes after 365 days unless you choose to make a new one-time purchase. It does not renew automatically.
  • Lifetime Premium Access: A one-time purchase granting perpetual access for the respective device. It does not renew automatically.
  • Entitlement Verification: Verified in real-time with Google Play. Plaintext purchase tokens are never stored in the database.

1.4 Payment Processing and Service Providers

  • Google Play In-App Billing (Android): For Android purchases, Google handles billing, payment processing, and currency collection. CozyTV+ does not receive, process, or store credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank account details. Google processes transaction data under its own privacy policies.
  • Lemon Squeezy (LG webOS): For LG webOS one-time purchases, Lemon Squeezy acts as authorized merchant of record under its own privacy policy.
  • Backend Infrastructure: Technical entitlement records and API endpoints operate on Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1, acting as technical data processors under our control.

1.5 Advertising, Analytics, and Diagnostics

  • No Advertising SDKs: CozyTV+ does not contain third-party advertising SDKs or deliver behavioral ads.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: CozyTV+ does not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or cross-app tracking mechanisms.
  • No Remote Crash Telemetry: CozyTV+ does not transmit personal crash dumps or background telemetry to remote servers.

1.6 Data Retention and Deletion Policy

You have full control over your data:

  • Local Device Data: You can wipe all local playlists, credentials, and viewing histories immediately by using Reset App in CozyTV+ settings or clearing application storage in operating system settings.
  • Backend Data Deletion Requests: You may request the permanent deletion and anonymization of your backend device record at any time by emailing htok2005@gmail.com with your CozyTV+ Device Code (e.g. CZTV-XXXX-XXXX). Users do not create user accounts in CozyTV+.
  • Processing Timeline: Requests are processed within 30 calendar days. If necessary for identity verification or ownership confirmation, additional non-sensitive verification details may be requested.
  • Deleted Data: Active Premium entitlement records, trial period records, legacy backend access records previously designated as subscriptions, checkout and session initiation records, all technical device metadata (device category, OS version, Android SDK/API level, manufacturer, model, and application version are permanently cleared to null, and platform is set to deleted), and the stored pseudonymized device identifier (permanently overwritten with an anonymous placeholder, e.g. deleted_<id>).
  • Retained Data: The device code is retained solely as an audit trail of the deletion request and anonymization. Limited Google Play order audit records (order number, transaction amount, currency code, payment status, completion timestamp) may be retained for up to 10 years strictly for statutory tax, commercial accounting, fraud prevention, refund, and chargeback obligations. Retained financial records are linked exclusively to the anonymized device record. Plaintext purchase tokens, raw Android identifiers, playlist URLs, and passwords are never persisted in the backend database.
  • Impact on Google Play Purchases: A data deletion request anonymizes the device record and removes trials, Premium entitlement, and access records. Deletion does NOT cancel your Google Play order and does NOT issue an automatic refund. Google Play orders and payment records are subject to Google's own retention policies. Restoring purchases upon reinstalling the application depends on real-time Google Play verification and product validity. Premium access linked to the deleted device code does not continue.
  • Dedicated Instructions: Please see our dedicated CozyTV+ Data Deletion Page for complete instructions.

2.2 Yalnızca Cihazınızda Yerel Olarak Saklanan Veriler (Toplanmayan Veriler)

Oynatıcı fonksiyonlarının çalışabilmesi için CozyTV+ şu yapılandırma verilerini yalnızca cihazınızın yerel hafızasında (yerel tarayıcı depolama alanı ve IndexedDB) saklar. Bu veriler CozyTV+ sunucularına asla iletilmez:

  • Çalma Listeleri ve Giriş Bilgileri: M3U/M3U8 URL'leri, Xtream sunucu adresleri, kullanıcı adları, parolalar ve XMLTV/EPG adresleri.
  • Tercihler ve Oynatma Durumu: Favori kanallar, özel kanal sıralamaları, ses/altyazı seçimleri, izleme ilerlemesi, hatırlatıcılar ve izleme listeleri.
  • Ebeveyn Denetimleri: Salt uygulanmış PIN özetleri ve kanal kısıtlama bayrakları.

Önbelleğe alınan geçici medya ve rehber verileri Önbelleği Temizle (Clear Cache) seçeneğiyle silinebilir. Kullanıcı tarafından eklenen hesaplar, listeler, kimlik bilgileri, tercihler ve geçmiş verileri; Ayarlar menüsünden Uygulamayı Sıfırla (Reset App) seçeneği kullanılana, işletim sistemi üzerinden uygulama verileri temizlenene veya CozyTV+ cihazdan kaldırılana kadar cihazda kalır.

CozyTV+ oynatıcı fonksiyonları için kullanıcı hesabı kaydı gerekmez. Kişisel hesap zorunluluğu olmaksızın deneme sürelerini yönetmek, teknik destek sağlamak, arayüzü cihaz türüne göre doğru sunmak ve Premium yetkilerini yönetebilmek amacıyla sunucumuzda sınırlı takma adlı teknik cihaz bilgileri işlenir:

A. Cihaz Tanımlayıcıları ve Teknik Cihaz Bilgileri

  • Android Takma Adlı Tanımlayıcı: Android üzerinde uygulama, Android tarafından sağlanan kararlı cihaz tanımlayıcısından türetilmiş, tek yönlü ve takma adlı bir cihaz kimliği kullanır. Android tarafından sağlanan kararlı tanımlayıcı, tek yönlü takma adlı cihaz kimliği türetmek amacıyla HTTPS üzerinden güvenli biçimde iletilir ve yalnızca geçici olarak işlenir. Ham Android tanımlayıcısı backend veritabanında kalıcı olarak saklanmaz ve loglanmaz. CozyTV+ kişisel cihaz numaraları veya kalıcı donanım profil özetleri toplamaz.
  • LG webOS Tanımlayıcısı: LG webOS platformunda LGUDID, yalnızca uygulama içinde verdiğiniz açık rıza sonrasında tek seferlik deneme kuralını uygulamak ve satın almaları geri yüklemek amacıyla işlenir.
  • CozyTV+ Cihaz Kodu: Sunucumuz tarafından üretilen ve cihaz yetkinizle ilişkilendirilen takma adlı bir kod (örn. CZTV-XXXX-XXXX).
  • Teknik Cihaz Bilgileri: Cihaz kaydı ve periyodik senkronizasyon sırasında CozyTV+ sunucusuna şu teknik cihaz bilgileri gönderilebilir:
    • Cihaz Kategorisi: Arayüzün doğru sunulabilmesi için cihaz sınıfı (telefon, tablet, Android TV, Google TV veya LG webOS TV).
    • İşletim Sistemi Sürümü: Android veya webOS işletim sistemi sürümü (örn. Android sürümü).
    • Android SDK/API Seviyesi: Sayısal Android SDK/API seviyesi (örn. API 31, 33, 34).
    • Cihaz Üreticisi: Donanım üreticisi adı (örn. Sony, Google, Samsung, LG).
    • Cihaz Modeli: Donanım model adı veya belirteci (örn. BRAVIA 4K, Pixel 8, Galaxy Tab S8, OLED55C1).

Bu teknik cihaz bilgileri ve tanımlayıcılar yalnızca şu amaçlarla kullanılır:
