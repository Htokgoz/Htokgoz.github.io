CozyTV+ Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

Applies to: CozyTV+ on LG webOS, Android, and iOS.

1. About CozyTV+ and Content Responsibility

CozyTV+ is a smart media player designed to play and organize audiovisual media from sources provided directly by the user. CozyTV+ does not provide, host, sell, broadcast, or distribute television channels, streams, playlists, or any media content. Users are solely responsible for ensuring they have legitimate rights and licenses to access any content or streams they configure.

CozyTV+ is developed and published under the brand name Cosmo Online.

  • Developer / Brand: Cosmo Online
  • Contact / Privacy Inquiries: htok2005@gmail.com

2. Data Stored Exclusively on Your Device (Not Collected)

To provide playback functionality, CozyTV+ stores the following configuration data locally on your device (using local browser storage and IndexedDB). This data is never transmitted to CozyTV+ servers:

  • Playlists and Account Credentials: M3U/M3U8 URLs, Xtream server addresses, usernames, passwords, and XMLTV/EPG URLs.
  • Preferences & Playback State: Favorite channels, custom channel ordering, audio/subtitle selections, playback progress, reminders, and watchlists.
  • Parental Controls: Salted PIN hashes and channel restriction flags.

Temporary cached media and guide data may be removed using Clear Cache. User-added accounts, playlists, credentials, preferences, favorites, and viewing history remain on the device until you use Reset App, clear the application's storage through the operating system, or uninstall CozyTV+.

3. Data Processed on Backend Servers (Collected Data)

CozyTV+ does not require an email or user account registration for playback. To manage free trial periods and premium entitlements without requiring personal accounts, our backend processes minimal pseudonymous device information:

3.1 Device Identifiers

  • LGUDID / Platform Device Identifier: On LG webOS, accessed only after your explicit in-app consent. Used solely to recognize the device, enforce single-use trial rules, and restore paid entitlements if the app is reinstalled.
  • CozyTV+ Device Code: A pseudonymous identifier (e.g., CZTV-XXXX) generated by our backend and associated with your device entitlement.
  • Technical Metadata: Platform name (e.g., webOS) and application version.

3.2 Entitlement and Transaction Lifecycle

  • Entitlement Metadata: The applicable premium plan or entitlement type (including subscription and one-time Lifetime entitlement verification), entitlement status, and validity period where applicable. Lifetime entitlements have no expiry date.
  • Transaction Identifiers: Internal transaction IDs and payment processor reference IDs (including Lemon Squeezy order identifiers for Lifetime purchases) used to verify and maintain entitlement state.

4. Payment Processing and Service Providers

  • Payment Processor (Merchant of Record): Premium purchases and subscriptions are processed by our authorized merchant of record, Lemon Squeezy. CozyTV+ does not collect, receive, process, or store credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank account details. Lemon Squeezy processes transaction data under its own privacy policy.
  • Backend Infrastructure: Our API and entitlement database operate on Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1, acting as technical data processors under our control.

5. Advertising, Analytics, and Diagnostics

  • No Advertising SDKs: CozyTV+ does not contain third-party advertising SDKs or deliver behavioral ads.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: CozyTV+ does not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or cross-app tracking mechanisms.
  • No Remote Crash Reporting: CozyTV+ does not automatically transmit crash dumps or telemetry to remote servers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

  • Local Device Data: Temporary cached media and guide data may be removed using Clear Cache. User-added accounts, playlists, credentials, preferences, favorites, and viewing history remain on the device until you use Reset App, clear the application's storage through the operating system, or uninstall CozyTV+.
  • Backend Entitlement & Trial Records: Pseudonymous device identifiers, trial records, and entitlement metadata are retained in our Cloudflare D1 database for as long as necessary to prevent repeat-trial abuse, maintain and restore premium entitlements, and operate the service. There is currently no automated database deletion schedule.
  • Manual Deletion Requests: You may request the deletion of your pseudonymous device record from our backend database at any time by emailing htok2005@gmail.com with your CozyTV+ Device Code.
  • Impact of Deletion: Deleting backend device records removes CozyTV+'s entitlement association for that device. This includes any Lifetime Premium entitlement association — the Lifetime entitlement may be lost if the backend device record is deleted. This is separate from subscription cancellation: cancelling a subscription stops future renewal charges; deleting a device record removes CozyTV+ backend data only. Lemon Squeezy merchant and order records (including Lifetime purchase records) are not automatically deleted. Backend deletion does not automatically cancel recurring billing.
